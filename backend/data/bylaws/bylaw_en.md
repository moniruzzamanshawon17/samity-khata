# Samity-Khata — Bylaw of Shapla Sanchay O Rindan Samabay Samity Ltd

SAMPLE / MODEL BYLAW FOR THE "Samity-Khata" SOFTWARE PROJECT. NOT A REGISTERED DOCUMENT.
Language: English. Each "####" heading is one clause and one RAG chunk.

#### Clause 1.1 — Name, office and status
The society is "Shapla Sanchay O Rindan Samabay Samity Ltd" (the Samity), a primary savings and credit cooperative under the Cooperative Societies Act 2001. Office: Village Nayapara, Shantinagar Union, Rupnagar Upazila (fictional). This is a SAMPLE bylaw for the "Samity-Khata" software project; it is not registered with the Department of Cooperatives.

#### Clause 1.2 — Definitions and money unit
"Act" means the Cooperative Societies Act 2001 as amended; "Rules" means the Cooperative Societies Rules 2004 as amended; "Registrar" means the Registrar of the Department of Cooperatives or an officer empowered by him. All amounts are in taka and are recorded in poisha (Tk 1 = 100 poisha). The cooperative year runs from 1 July to 30 June.

#### Clause 1.3 — Objectives and working area
The Samity collects shares and monthly savings from members and lends only to members for productive and essential needs. Its membership and working area is Shantinagar Union. Under section 26(1) of the Act, the Samity shall not accept deposits from, or give loans to, any non-member.

#### Clause 2.1 — Membership eligibility
Any Bangladeshi citizen aged 18 or above, of sound mind, living or working in Shantinagar Union, may apply in writing. The applicant must not be a defaulter of this Samity. Admission needs Managing Committee approval, the applicant's signature or thumbprint in the member register, and a written promise to obey the Act, Rules and this bylaw.

#### Clause 2.2 — Admission fee and shares
Each new member pays a one-time, non-refundable admission fee of Tk 100 and buys at least 2 shares of Tk 500 each (Tk 1,000). No member may hold more than one-fifth of total share capital (section 15(2) of the Act). The Samity does not buy back shares; they may only be transferred to another or new member with Committee consent (section 15(3)-(4)).

#### Clause 2.3 — Member number
On admission the Secretary assigns a unique member number in the form SSR-0001, in serial order. A member number is never changed or reused, even after exit or death. Every deposit, loan, fine and receipt is recorded against this number.

#### Clause 2.4 — Active and inactive status
A member is "active" when not more than 2 monthly deposits are unpaid. A member with 3 or more unpaid monthly deposits becomes "inactive". An inactive member cannot receive a new loan or be a guarantor, and becomes active again on paying all arrears and fines. After exit or death (Clause 2.6) the status is "exited".

#### Clause 2.5 — Nominee
Each member shall nominate in writing one adult person to receive the member's savings balance and shares (by transfer) after death. The member may change or cancel the nomination at any time in writing; the latest nomination recorded by the Secretary prevails. Nominee details are confidential under Clause 6.4.

#### Clause 2.6 — Exit, death and expulsion
A member with no outstanding loan or guarantee may resign with 30 days' written notice. On death, the nominee claims with a death certificate. Within 30 days of approval the Samity pays the savings balance less any dues; shares are transferred, not bought back. The Committee may expel a member for wilful breach of this bylaw or loan default over 6 months, after 7 days' notice and a hearing, subject to general meeting approval.

#### Clause 3.1 — Monthly deposit amount and due date
Every member shall deposit a fixed compulsory saving of Tk 500 (50,000 poisha) per month, the same for all members. Each month's deposit is due on or before the 10th of that month. Advance deposits are allowed and are recorded month by month. Only a general meeting may change the amount.

#### Clause 3.2 — Late monthly deposit (what happens if a deposit is paid late)
A monthly deposit not paid by the 10th is late. On the 11th a late fine of Tk 10 is charged for each unpaid deposit, plus Tk 10 for every further 30 days it stays unpaid, up to Tk 50 per deposit. The fine is Samity income, recorded as a separate entry, and is never deducted from savings. Three unpaid deposits make the member inactive (Clause 2.4).

#### Clause 3.3 — Savings balance
A member's savings balance = the total of all savings deposits recorded for that member minus all savings withdrawals and adjustments paid out. Admission fees, share money, fines and loan instalments are not part of the savings balance. The balance is always calculated from the ledger and is never typed in by hand.

#### Clause 3.4 — Corrections only by reversing entries
No financial entry may ever be deleted or edited. A wrong entry is corrected only by a reversing entry of equal and opposite amount, followed by the correct entry. Each reversal records the reason, date and the Secretary who approved it. The original entry stays visible in the ledger and audit trail.

#### Clause 3.5 — Savings withdrawal
A member may withdraw up to 50% of the savings balance once in any 12 months, with 15 days' written notice. While the member has an active or overdue loan, or is a guarantor, the balance after withdrawal must not fall below one-third of the outstanding principal. The full balance is paid only on exit (Clause 2.6).

#### Clause 3.6 — Liquidity
The Samity keeps at least 25% of total members' savings in its account at a scheduled bank or Bangladesh Samabaya Bank, and lends at most 75% of savings. Cash in hand above Tk 20,000 is banked within 2 working days. Investments follow section 33 of the Act and the Rules.

#### Clause 4.1 — Loan eligibility
A member may apply for a loan if he or she: (a) has been a member for at least 6 months; (b) is active with no unpaid monthly deposit; (c) has no other active or overdue loan from the Samity; and (d) is not guarantor of an overdue loan. Committee members have the same loan rights as ordinary members and no more (section 26 of the Act).

#### Clause 4.2 — Loan ceiling
The maximum loan is the lowest of: (a) 3 times the member's savings balance on the application date; (b) 40 times the member's paid-up share capital, as required by rule 74(1) of the Rules; and (c) Tk 50,000. Example: savings Tk 7,000 and shares Tk 1,000 give a ceiling of Tk 21,000.

#### Clause 4.3 — Loan purposes
Loans are given only for: (a) small business or trade; (b) agriculture, livestock, poultry or fisheries; (c) education fees and materials; (d) medical treatment; (e) repair or improvement of the member's dwelling; (f) tools or equipment for earning a living. The purpose must be stated on the application. Loans for gambling, speculation or unlawful activity are prohibited.

#### Clause 4.4 — Application and approval authority
A member applies on the prescribed form stating amount, purpose, term and guarantor. Only the Managing Committee, by a decision recorded in its meeting minutes, may approve a loan. The Secretary then creates the loan record with the approved amount, rate, term and schedule. No loan may be given without a Committee decision.

#### Clause 4.5 — Guarantor
Each loan needs one guarantor who is an active member, has a savings balance of at least one-quarter of the loan amount, and has no overdue loan. A member may guarantee only one loan at a time. If the borrower defaults, the guarantor is liable for the overdue amount under Clause 5.3.

#### Clause 4.6 — Interest rate and method
Loan interest is 12% per year, charged as simple interest on the reducing (declining) balance: 1% per month on the principal outstanding. The flat method is not used. Each month's interest is calculated to the nearest poisha (half rounded up). Only a general meeting may change the rate, and a change applies to new loans only.

#### Clause 4.7 — Equal monthly instalment and rounding
Instalment A = P × r ÷ [1 − (1 + r)^−n], where P = principal, r = 0.01 and n = number of months. A is rounded UP to the next whole taka. Each payment first covers that month's interest, then principal. The last instalment equals remaining principal plus interest, so it may be slightly smaller. Example: Tk 20,000 for 12 months = Tk 1,777 × 11, then Tk 1,776.70.

#### Clause 4.8 — Term, due date and early repayment
Loan terms are 6, 12, 18 or 24 months. Instalments are due on the 10th of each month, the first falling at least 15 days after disbursement. A borrower may prepay all or part at any time without penalty; prepayment reduces principal, the instalment stays the same and the term becomes shorter, and interest is charged only on principal outstanding.

#### Clause 5.1 — Loan status and overdue definition
Every loan has one status: "active" (repaid on schedule), "overdue" or "closed" (fully repaid). A loan becomes overdue when any instalment remains unpaid more than 15 days after its due date (15-day grace period). It returns to active when all overdue instalments and fines are paid, and is closed when principal, interest and fines are fully paid.

#### Clause 5.2 — Late-payment fine on loan instalments
If an instalment is still unpaid after the 15-day grace period (on the 16th day after the due date), a fine of Tk 20 is charged for that instalment, plus Tk 20 for every further 30 days it stays unpaid, up to Tk 100 per instalment. No interest is charged on fines. Fines are recorded as separate entries and are collected before the overdue instalment.

#### Clause 5.3 — Recovery of overdue loans
If a loan is overdue for more than 90 days, the Committee may, after 15 days' written notice, adjust the overdue amount from the borrower's savings balance and then from the guarantor's savings balance. Remaining dues may be recovered under the Act and Rules through the Registrar. Every adjustment is a new recorded entry, never an edit.

#### Clause 6.1 — Secretary (administrator) powers
The Secretary administers the records. The Secretary may add and edit member details; record deposits, withdrawals, instalments and fines; create loans approved by the Committee; and make reversing entries under Clause 3.4. The Secretary may delegate data entry but remains responsible. The Treasurer checks cash and bank balances every month.

#### Clause 6.2 — Collector (staff) powers and limits
A collector appointed by the Committee may record monthly deposits and loan instalments received and must issue a receipt for each. A collector may not create or approve loans, add or edit members, record withdrawals or make reversals. A collector's error is reported to the Secretary the same day and corrected under Clause 3.4.

#### Clause 6.3 — What a member can see
Each member has the right to see his or her own savings balance, deposit history, loan schedule, payments, dues and fines, in the passbook, at the office or through the Samity's software. A member can see only his or her own records. The Samity gives a written statement within 7 days of a request.

#### Clause 6.4 — Confidentiality of member data
Any member's savings, loans, dues, nominee, address and phone number are confidential. They shall not be disclosed to any other member or outsider, except to the member, the nominee after death, auditors, the Registrar or a court as the law requires. Office-bearers or staff who breach this may be removed. General meetings receive totals only, not individual data.

#### Clause 6.5 — Registers and receipts
The Samity keeps the registers required by section 24 of the Act and the Rules — member register, share register, savings ledger, loan ledger, cash book and minutes book — on paper or electronically. Every cash receipt gets a serially numbered receipt. Records are kept for at least 10 years.

#### Clause 7.1 — Managing Committee
The Samity is run by a 6-member Managing Committee: President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer and 2 members, elected by the general meeting under the Rules. The elected Committee serves 3 years from its first meeting; the first Committee approved at registration serves 2 years (section 18 of the Act). Candidates must be at least 21 and members for 12 months (section 19).

#### Clause 7.2 — Committee meetings
The Committee meets at least once a month. The quorum is 4 of 6 members. Decisions are taken by majority and recorded in the minutes book. Loan approvals, admissions, resignations and expulsions are decided only in Committee meetings. No Committee member takes part in deciding his or her own loan.

#### Clause 7.3 — General meetings
The Annual General Meeting is held within 60 days of completion of audit (section 17(3) of the Act) and approves accounts, budget, profit distribution and elections. Notice with the audited balance sheet is sent at least 15 days before. Quorum is one-third of members when there are 100 or fewer (section 17(5)). A special general meeting is called on written request of one-third of members (section 17(8)).

#### Clause 7.4 — Profit distribution and reserve fund
From each cooperative year's net profit, after first setting 50% against past losses if any: (a) at least 15% to the Reserve Fund; (b) 10% to a bad and doubtful loan fund; (c) 3% Cooperative Development Fund contribution; (d) 5% to a member education and welfare fund; (e) the rest as dividend on shares (section 34 of the Act).

#### Clause 7.5 — Audit
The accounts are audited at least once every cooperative year by the Department of Cooperatives or an auditor appointed under section 43 of the Act. Defects shown in the audit report are corrected within 60 days. The Samity pays the audit fee fixed under the Rules.

#### Clause 7.6 — Dispute resolution
Any dispute about the Samity's business between members, the Committee, staff or the Samity that the Committee cannot settle within 30 days shall be referred to the Registrar under section 50 of the Act and decided as provided in the Act and Rules.

#### Clause 7.7 — Amendment and matters not covered
This bylaw may be amended only by a general meeting and takes effect after approval by the Registrar (section 13 of the Act). Matters not covered here are governed by the Act and Rules. If asked about a matter this bylaw does not mention, the answer is that the bylaw makes no provision for it.
